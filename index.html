EnterprEase Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

EnterprEase is a general business productivity application designed to help users manage sales, purchases, inventory, schedules, and business records. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the application.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features used, EnterprEase may process account details such as a username, email address, and profile information, as well as sales transactions, purchases, product details, inventory quantities, barcode values, and scheduled events.

3. Camera Permission

The barcode and QR code scanning feature may access your device camera when you choose to scan an item. Camera access is used to read product codes. You may revoke camera permission through your device settings, but scanning will not work without it.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to support account access, maintain business records, manage inventory, process sales and purchases, calculate totals, display analytics, manage schedules, and save profile settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

Depending on the implemented feature, information may be stored locally on your device using application storage or secure storage. Users should protect their devices and credentials. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Sharing of Information

EnterprEase does not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the application and its users. If third-party services are integrated, their privacy practices may also apply.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information may remain on your device until it is deleted or application data is cleared. Uninstalling the application or clearing its data may remove locally stored records. Keep backups of important business information where appropriate.

8. Children's Privacy

EnterprEase is intended for general business productivity and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Subject to applicable laws, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information where applicable. Users may also manage camera permission through device settings.

10. Third-Party Services

Future versions may use third-party libraries or services. This policy should be updated whenever new services, online storage, analytics, or advertising tools are introduced.

11. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, data practices, or applicable laws change. The effective date should be updated whenever a revised policy is published.
